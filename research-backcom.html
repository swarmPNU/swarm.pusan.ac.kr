Full-Duplex BackCom

SWARM Laboratory investigates low-power and self-sustainable wireless communication systems based on backscatter communication. Our research covers communication architectures, channel estimation, integrated sensing, and intelligent signal processing for next-generation IoT systems.

Full-Duplex Backscatter Communication

We investigate full-duplex backscatter communication systems for low-power wireless connectivity. The research focuses on practical communication architectures and signal-processing techniques for improving communication range, reliability, and spectral efficiency while maintaining the low-power characteristics of backscatter devices.

Integrated Sensing and Backscatter Communication

We explore the integration of sensing and backscatter communication for self-sustainable wireless systems. By combining sensing and communication functions within a low-power platform, the research aims to support autonomous IoT devices capable of acquiring and transmitting information with minimal energy consumption.

Intelligent Backscatter Communication

AI and machine-learning techniques are investigated for intelligent backscatter communication systems. Our research includes phase-aware channel estimation, adaptive signal processing, and data-driven optimization methods for improving communication performance under practical wireless-channel variations.

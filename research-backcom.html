Full-Duplex BackCom

Overview

Low-power and self-sustainable wireless communication

Our research explores backscatter communication technologies for low-power and self-sustainable wireless systems. By utilizing reflected radio-frequency signals for information transmission, backscatter communication can significantly reduce the power required by conventional active wireless transmitters.

Research Description

Intelligent backscatter communication systems

We investigate full-duplex backscatter architectures that enable simultaneous wireless operation while addressing challenges associated with interference, channel variation, and phase uncertainty. System-level approaches are studied to improve communication reliability and extend the practical operating range of backscatter links.

Our research also combines backscatter communication with sensing and intelligent signal processing. AI and machine learning techniques are investigated for channel estimation, signal processing, and adaptive wireless operation, with the goal of realizing energy-efficient and autonomous IoT communication and sensing platforms.

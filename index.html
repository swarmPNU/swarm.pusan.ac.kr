ABOUT SWARM

Exploring the Future of Wireless Technology

The Smart Wireless Antenna, Radiation, and Microwave Laboratory at Pusan National University conducts research in applied electromagnetics, antennas, microwave and millimeter-wave technologies, and advanced wireless systems.

OUR RESEARCH

Research Areas

01

Antennas & Arrays

Advanced antenna arrays, beam steering, reflectarrays, and reconfigurable antenna systems.

02

RF & mmWave

Microwave, millimeter-wave and sub-THz RF components and wireless systems.

03

Antenna-in-Package

Multilayer RF packaging and highly integrated antenna-in-package technologies.

04

Emerging Wireless

RF photonics, sensing, advanced materials, and next-generation wireless technologies.

Antenna-in-Package (AiP)

Overview

Highly integrated RF front-end and antenna systems

Our research investigates antenna-in-package technologies for compact and highly integrated next-generation wireless systems. Antennas, RF passive components, beamforming circuits, signal-routing networks, and package structures are co-designed to realize high-performance RF front-end modules within a compact form factor.

Research Description

Multilayer integration for next-generation wireless modules

We investigate multilayer RF packaging technologies including low-temperature co-fired ceramic (LTCC) platforms for integrating antenna arrays and RF front-end components. Multilayer structures enable compact vertical integration of antennas, ground planes, RF transmission lines, DC routing, and interconnection structures within a single package.

Our research includes the integration of beamforming ICs with antenna arrays and passive RF networks to realize electronically steerable antenna modules. RF signal distribution, package interconnection, and control-board interfaces are considered together to improve the electrical performance and integration density of the overall system.

Thermal management is also investigated for highly integrated RF modules. Thermal vias and internal metal structures can provide heat-conduction and spreading paths through multilayer packages, helping reduce localized heat accumulation around active RF components.

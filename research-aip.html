Antenna-in-Package (AiP)

SWARM Laboratory develops highly integrated Antenna-in-Package technologies for next-generation wireless systems. Our research combines antenna arrays, RF front-end components, beamforming ICs, multilayer packaging, and thermal-management technologies within compact RF modules.

LTCC-Based Antenna-in-Package

We develop multilayer Antenna-in-Package structures using low-temperature co-fired ceramic technology. The multilayer platform enables compact integration of antennas, RF interconnections, feeding networks, and other RF front-end components.

Integrated RF Front-End Components

RF front-end components including antennas, filters, filtennas, and multilayer feeding networks are investigated for highly integrated wireless modules. The research focuses on compact integration while maintaining RF and radiation performance.

Beam-Steering Antenna-in-Package

We integrate antenna arrays, beamforming ICs, RF routing, control interfaces, and thermal structures within a multilayer package to realize compact beam-steering RF front-end modules for upper-mid-band and next-generation wireless applications.

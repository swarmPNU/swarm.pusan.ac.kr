Research at SWARM

Advanced electromagnetic technologies for next-generation wireless systems.

The Smart Wireless Antenna, Radiation, and Microwave (SWARM) Laboratory conducts research in applied electromagnetics, wireless technologies, RF, and millimeter-wave systems. Our research focuses on advanced antenna and electromagnetic technologies for next-generation communication, sensing, and integrated wireless systems.

Phased Array Image
01 / PHASED ARRAY

Phased Array

We investigate phased-array antenna technologies for high-performance wireless systems requiring electronic beam steering and adaptive radiation control. Our research includes array antenna design, beamforming architectures, reconfigurable antenna systems, and RF front-end integration for microwave and millimeter-wave applications.

Research Topics
  • Phased-array antennas
  • Electronic beam steering
  • Beamforming systems
  • Reconfigurable antennas
  • Wide-angle scanning
  • Microwave & mmWave arrays
02 / FULL-DUPLEX BACKCOM

Full-Duplex BackCom

We develop low-power and self-sustainable wireless communication technologies based on backscatter communication. Our research explores full-duplex backscatter architectures, channel estimation, interference management, sensing and communication, and intelligent signal processing for future Internet-of-Things systems.

Research Topics
  • Backscatter communication
  • Full-duplex BackCom
  • Self-sustainable wireless systems
  • Channel estimation
  • Integrated sensing & communication
  • AI/ML-assisted communication
03 / ABSORBER & METASURFACE

Magnetic Materials-Based Absorber & Metasurface

We investigate magnetic materials and engineered electromagnetic surfaces for controlling the propagation, reflection, transmission, and absorption of electromagnetic waves. Our research includes broadband absorbers, multifunctional metasurfaces, and reconfigurable electromagnetic structures for microwave and millimeter-wave applications.

Research Topics
  • Magnetic microwave absorbers
  • Broadband EM absorption
  • Multifunctional metasurfaces
  • Reconfigurable reflectarrays
  • Polarization control
  • EM-wave manipulation
04 / ANTENNA-IN-PACKAGE

Antenna-in-Package (AiP)

We develop highly integrated antenna-in-package technologies for compact RF front-end systems. Our research focuses on the integration of antenna arrays, passive RF components, beamforming circuits, and multilayer packaging technologies for next-generation wireless applications.

Research Topics
  • Antenna-in-Package
  • LTCC RF packaging
  • RF front-end integration
  • Beam-steering antenna arrays
  • Integrated passive components
  • Thermal management

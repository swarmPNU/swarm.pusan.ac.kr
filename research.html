Research at SWARM

Advanced electromagnetic technologies for next-generation wireless systems.

The Smart Wireless Antenna, Radiation, and Microwave (SWARM) Laboratory conducts research in applied electromagnetics, wireless technologies, RF, and millimeter-wave systems. Our research focuses on advanced antenna and electromagnetic technologies for next-generation communication, sensing, and integrated wireless systems.

01

Phased Array

We investigate phased-array antenna technologies for high-performance wireless systems requiring electronic beam steering, wide-angle scanning, and adaptive radiation control.

View Details →
02

Backscatter Communication

We investigate advanced backscatter communication technologies for high-speed and reliable wireless connectivity, including MIMO backscatter, robust signal processing, and AI-driven intelligent wireless systems.

View Details →
03

Magnetic Materials-Based
Absorber & Metasurface

We investigate ferrite-based absorbers and multifunctional metasurfaces for microwave absorption, reflection, transmission, and electromagnetic-wave control under varying environmental conditions.

View Details →
04

Antenna-in-Package (AiP)

We develop highly integrated antenna and RF front-end technologies using multilayer packaging platforms for compact beamforming and next-generation wireless systems.

Overview

Electromagnetic materials and surfaces for wave control

Our research investigates magnetic materials, electromagnetic absorbers, and engineered metasurfaces for controlling the interaction between electromagnetic waves and materials. The research aims to realize compact and multifunctional structures capable of manipulating absorption, reflection, radiation, and polarization characteristics.

Research Description

Advanced electromagnetic wave manipulation

Magnetic materials provide additional degrees of freedom for controlling electromagnetic-wave absorption and propagation. We investigate material-based and structurally engineered approaches for realizing broadband and high-performance electromagnetic absorbers at microwave and millimeter-wave frequencies.

Our research also explores multifunctional and reconfigurable metasurfaces capable of controlling reflection, radiation, beam direction, and polarization. These technologies can be applied to advanced antennas, radar systems, interference mitigation, and future wireless communication platforms.

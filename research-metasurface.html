Magnetic Materials-Based
Absorber & Metasurface

SWARM Laboratory investigates magnetic materials, electromagnetic absorbers, and multifunctional metasurfaces for controlling electromagnetic-wave absorption, reflection, transmission, and radiation. Our research also investigates robust electromagnetic performance under temperature-varying environments for advanced wireless and satellite applications.

01

Ferrite-Based Microwave Absorber

We investigate microwave absorbers based on magnetic ferrite materials and meta-composite structures. The electromagnetic properties of the magnetic material and absorber structure are engineered to achieve effective microwave absorption and reflection characteristics.

02

Temperature-Robust Electromagnetic Performance

We investigate the temperature-dependent electromagnetic characteristics of ferrite-based structures, including changes in microwave absorption and reflection performance. This research aims to maintain stable RF operation under temperature-varying environments.

03

Multifunctional Ferrite-Based Metasurface

We develop multifunctional metasurfaces using W-type ferrite materials for integrated electromagnetic-wave control. The metasurface enables multiple functions including beam reflection, transmission, and microwave absorption within a single electromagnetic platform.

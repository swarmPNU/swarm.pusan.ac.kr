Magnetic Materials-Based
Absorber & Metasurface

SWARM Laboratory investigates magnetic materials, electromagnetic absorbers, and engineered metasurfaces for controlling electromagnetic-wave propagation, reflection, absorption, polarization, and radiation characteristics across microwave and millimeter-wave frequency bands.

Magnetic Materials-Based Electromagnetic Absorber

We investigate broadband electromagnetic absorbers using ferrites and magnetic composite materials. Material properties and absorber geometries are engineered to achieve high absorption performance over microwave and millimeter-wave frequency bands.

Multifunctional Metasurface

Multifunctional metasurfaces are investigated for manipulating electromagnetic waves within compact planar structures. The research includes wave absorption, radiation control, beam manipulation, and polarization control using engineered electromagnetic surfaces.

Reconfigurable Electromagnetic Surface

We develop reconfigurable electromagnetic surfaces capable of dynamically controlling reflected and radiated electromagnetic waves. The research focuses on beam steering, polarization control, and adaptive electromagnetic-wave manipulation for advanced wireless systems.
